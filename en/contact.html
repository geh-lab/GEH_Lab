Transportation

Public transit and driving guidance for visitors heading to College of Agriculture and Life Sciences 1 (E10-1).

Bus
City

East gate (College of Agriculture and Life Sciences): 105, 115

Main gate: 101, 102, 105, 106, 113, 114, 115, 121, 704

West gate: 704, 912

Campus entry: 48, 108

Rail

Daejeon Station: Take bus 101 from Mokcheokgyo stop and get off at the main gate.

Seodaejeon Station: Take bus 101 or 119 from Seodaejeonnegeori Station stop and get off at the main gate.

Terminal

Yuseong Kumho Express Terminal: Take bus 104 from Jangdaenegori stop and get off at the main gate, or walk about 20 minutes.

Subway
Line 1

Yuseong Spa (CNU·Mokwon Univ.) Station, Exit 7

* After exiting, take a taxi or the campus shuttle bus.

* Buses via Yuseong Spa Station: East gate 105, 115 / Main gate 101, 102, 105, 106, 113, 114, 115, 121, 704 / West gate 704, 912

* The campus shuttle stops in front of the College of Agriculture and Life Sciences.

By car
Yuseong IC
Turn right toward Yuseong Spa → go straight about 1 km → stay in the right lane instead of entering the underpass → enter Chungnam National University through the main gate.
Or turn left at the first intersection and enter through the west gate.
Bukdaejeon IC
After exiting the IC, turn left → turn right → drive straight about 3 km → enter Chungnam National University through the west gate.
Inside campus
College of Agriculture and Life Sciences 1 is building E10-1, located on the east-gate side of the Daedeok campus. From the main gate or west gate, continue toward the east-gate area for the shortest route.
