Environmental Horticulture Science laboratory

R&D Projects

Welcome to the Environmental Horticulture Science laboratory!

Our laboratory focuses on studying the physiological and metabolic responses of functional crops using advanced plant factory and smart farm systems. In particular, we aim to enhance secondary metabolite production and understand environmental responses of high-value crops such as Cannabis sativa.

We are also developing an AI- and digital twin-based cultivation environment prediction and control platform, pursuing eco-friendly and data-driven next-generation agricultural technologies.

Current Research Projects

Indoor Medicinal Cannabis Standard Model

Establishment of the standard model for indoor precision production and quality control in medicinal cannabis.

Rooftop Greenhouse System

Development and demonstration of building-integrated rooftop greenhouse systems using distributed polygeneration.

Precision Water and Nutrient Supply System

Development of a precision water and nutrient supply system to enhance productivity and quality of horticultural crops.

AI-based Agricultural Water Management Platform

Development of an AI-driven platform for optimizing agricultural water management and improving sustainability.

AI-based Artificial Light Control Platform

Development of an AI-based artificial lighting control platform for smart farms to optimize light intensity and spectrum.

Greenhouse Waste Recycling Technology

Establishment of greenhouse cultivation waste recycling technology and assessment of its environmental and economic impact.
